Interview me about every aspect of this idea until we reach a precise and shared understanding.
Ask me clarifying questions to resolve ambiguities, one at a time.

- provide multiple alternative options,
- justify each option,
- explain the tradeoffs of each option,
- indicate your recommended option,
- identify any assumptions or unresolved dependencies.

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore the codebase instead.
If a question can be answered by exploring the documentation (`./*.md`), explore the documentation instead.
If a question can be answered by exploring the wiki (`~/repositorios/wiki`), explore the wiki instead.
If a question can be answered by exploring the style guide (`http://islas.dev/guia_de_estilo/`), explore the style guide instead.

Treat the conversation as a collaborative process of refining and pressure-testing the idea until it can be expressed clearly, precisely, and unambiguously to another person.

Wait for my answer before asking the next question.
If you repeat a question or ask a similar question, it means we have reached a shared understanding and we can end the interview.
